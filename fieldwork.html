Mission

Fieldwork gets early-stage, community-rooted work the room and resources it needs before anyone else will bet on it. Most funding shows up after an idea has already proven itself. We're interested in before.

That means hosting cooperative hackathons and community-led tech events, and offering the first, smallest checks and spaces to people building regenerative ventures — the kind of support that's hardest to find and matters most.

Current directions

Not all of this is funded yet — some of it is a sketch, not a commitment.
01

Seed support for emerging practice

Small, early checks and hosting for people prototyping new ways of working, at the point where an idea needs room more than resources.

02

Entrepreneurial incubation

Light, hands-on support for people building regenerative ventures — structure and feedback, not a program to graduate from.

03

Community tech convenings

Cooperative hackathons and build-days where local groups and technologists work on shared problems together, rather than for a client.

04

Warm data gatherings

Sessions that hold the relationships and context a spreadsheet can't capture — the information that lives between the numbers, not in them.

05

Published writing & media

Essays, audio, and film that carry the work into public conversation, beyond the room a gathering can hold.

About

Fieldwork is an independent practice, leading its own program design, partnerships, and funding.

Fieldwork shares roots with Limicelia, a sibling practice studio, though the two are run independently.
