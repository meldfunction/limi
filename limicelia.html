When we narrow the complexity of our problems,
we narrow the potential of our solutions.

“We believe many of our greatest challenges stem from a paradigm of oversimplification and reductionism, of solutionism and optimization. It flattens what it studies without ever living inside it.”

Who We Are

We are a practice studio focused on attending to what's alive in our communities and organizations. We create, we consult, and we cultivate spaces for receptivity and dialogue to make room for deeper engagement through creativity, regeneration, and awareness.

Our work moves across the personal, the interpersonal, and the systemic, so that we can work and live more fully together.

Three ways in

Each carries its own focus and partners, and each is one part of the same practice.
I.

Create

Writing, gatherings, and other forms that give shape to what's shifting — in how work, life, and living are understood.

II.

Consult

Coaching, trainings, and engagements with organizations navigating growth, transition, or strain — paced to what a group is ready for.

III.

Cultivate

Experiential practice for individual and collective awareness — contemplative, creative, and strategic, held at once.
