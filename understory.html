Understory

Weaving what's beneath the surface into shared understanding.

For over a decade, this practice began as research — studying how meaning, trust, and communication move differently across cultures inside organizations. That research became a question that outgrew the academy: what actually lets a group understand itself?

Understory is the practice that grew from asking it differently. We facilitate spaces — contemplative, creative, and strategic, held together rather than apart — where people and groups learn to sense what's actually happening beneath the surface of how they work.

This is slow work, on purpose.
Ways to Practice

What we hold

·

Practice cohorts

Small, sustained groups who bring a real challenge from their own work and stay with it together over weeks, not a workshop that ends when the day does.

·

Open practice sessions

Free, drop-in gatherings for anyone curious what it's like to sense what's actually alive in their work and life, no prior commitment required.

·

Dialogue and listening circles

Small-group formats, drawn from group relations and dialogue traditions, built for deep listening — where a group learns to stay with disagreement rather than resolve it too quickly.

·

Writing circles

Groups that shape personal or organizational experience into shared language, so what's been sensed individually becomes something a team can work with together.

·

Collective sensemaking circles

Structured conversation that lets a community sit with a shared question — often across real differences in background or culture — long enough for its shape to change.

·

Contemplative & awareness practice

Individual and group practice for presence, attention, and self-understanding — contemplative, creative, and strategic, held at once.

About

Understory is an independent practice, leading its own program design, partnerships, and funding.

Understory shares roots and some early collaborators with Limicelia, a sibling practice studio, though the two are run independently.
