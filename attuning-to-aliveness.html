Attuning to Aliveness

An afternoon of collective and individual practice — exploring what feels alive in ourselves and each other.

Stewarded by Carol, Lucy & Maja
Our Intention

We want to explore more deeply into what feels alive in individuals and collectives. There are many ways of knowing and connecting to our life forces — through relational practice, movement, sound, imagery, touch, taste, and beyond.

We want to cultivate a spacious space for all of us to explore different ways of knowing, connect more deeply with our aliveness, and form authentic relationships with each other. Over time, a true community — where self-authorship is respected and mutual support is nurtured — can emerge.

We believe this work can take many forms, and we invite offerings, ideas, and collaborations from the community.

Lineages & Disciplines We Draw From
Contemplative traditions — Tibetan & Zen Buddhism, Advaita Vedanta, Daoism Relational practices — T-group, Circling, Collective Presencing, Bohm Dialogue Healing modalities — Tai Chi, somatic healing, transpersonal psychology Creative modalities — mandala drawing, mystical poetry, authentic movement Regenerative system practices — social permaculture
The Flow of an Afternoon
1

Greetings & Centering

We start at 1:30pm with group meditation or movement to ground and relax, opening space for deeper connection with ourselves, each other, and the moment — followed by a brief round of check-in.

2

Collective Attunement — 1 hour

Relational practices such as Circling or T-group, attuning to what's alive in the here and now, in individuals and the collective. A brief introduction is offered for anyone new to these forms.

3

Individual Attunement — 1 hour

Facilitated and self-guided practices across movement, dialogue, sound, and imagery. Join, initiate, or practice solo. We observe the law of two feet: anyone may leave a practice at any time, no explanation needed.

4

Sacred Circle: Reflection & Integration

We return to a large circle to reflect on what we've noticed and learned, and explore how to integrate it into the month ahead. We close with hugs and goodbyes.

What We're Practicing
